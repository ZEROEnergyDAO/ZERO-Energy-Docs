## Español

ZERO Energy es una **red de infraestructura física descentralizada (DePIN) aplicada al sector energético**, desplegada en la red **Base**. Conecta instalaciones reales de energía renovable con una economía digital verificable: la producción que valida *Proof of Energy* queda registrada on-chain, y cada MWh validado equivale a 1 **ZERO REC (ZREC)**. **ZERO DAO (ZDAO)** es el token de gobernanza.

*Proof of Energy* es la capa que valida la producción energética. El consenso de la red lo pone Base: PoE no es un mecanismo de consenso.

## English

ZERO Energy is a **decentralized physical infrastructure network (DePIN) applied to the energy sector**, deployed on the **Base** network. It connects real renewable energy installations with a verifiable digital economy: production validated through *Proof of Energy* is recorded on-chain, and each validated MWh equals 1 **ZERO REC (ZREC)**. **ZERO DAO (ZDAO)** is the governance token.

*Proof of Energy* is the layer that validates energy production. Network consensus comes from Base: PoE is not a consensus mechanism.
